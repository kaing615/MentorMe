# MentorMe Domain Glossary

## User

An account that can authenticate with MentorMe. A User acts as a Mentor, Mentee, or Administrator according to its assigned role.

## Mentor

A User role that offers mentoring availability, accepts mentoring bookings, and may publish courses.

## Mentee

A User role that discovers mentors and courses, requests bookings, purchases courses, and participates in mentoring conversations.

## Profile

The public and professional information associated with a User, including skills, goals, languages, experience, and social links.

## Availability

A Mentor's dated collection of time slots that may be open, temporarily held, booked, blocked, or pending.

## Relationship

The mentoring association between one Mentor and one Mentee. Bookings and permitted mentoring communication occur in the context of this association.

## Booking

A request for a Mentee and Mentor to meet during a specific Availability slot. Its lifecycle records acceptance, rejection, completion, or cancellation.

## Course

Learning content published by a Mentor and offered to Mentees for purchase or enrolment.

## Order

A commercial record for purchasing one or more Courses or a mentoring Booking. It owns the payable amount and payment lifecycle.

## Purchased Course

A Mentee's durable entitlement to a Course after a successful Order, including learning progress and completion state.

## Message

A persisted text or attachment sent from one User to another within MentorMe's realtime conversation experience.
